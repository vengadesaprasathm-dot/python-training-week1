# Employee Data Analysis – Findings Summary

## 1. Problem
The project explores employee information to understand workforce patterns, compare average monthly income across departments, and identify areas that may need management attention.

## 2. Data
The analysis uses the Task 2 employee CSV. The original file contains **30 rows** and **10 columns**. After removing rows missing department, age, or monthly income, **30 records** were used for the numeric comparisons. Monthly income is expressed in the dataset's currency units.

## 3. Three Key Insights
1. **Department income:** IT has the highest average monthly income (**61,500.00**), while Sales has the lowest (**38,333.33**).
2. **Employee profile:** Average employee age is **33.6 years** and median monthly income is **46,500.00 currency units**.
3. **Workforce measure:** Overall attrition in the available records is **26.7%**.

## 4. Recommendations
1. Review compensation and role structure in **Sales**, whose average monthly income (38,333) is below **IT** (61,500); compare job levels and responsibilities before deciding on any adjustment.
2. Review exit reasons and retention risks using a larger, regularly updated employee dataset; use the observed attrition rate as a monitoring baseline.

## 5. Limitations
- The dataset is a small practice sample and may not represent the full workforce.
- The analysis is descriptive; it identifies patterns and does not prove cause and effect.
- Some fields may be missing or simplified, and monthly income currency is not explicitly specified in the dataset.
- Recommendations should be validated with HR context, role levels, employee feedback, and a larger dataset before real decisions are made.
